Welcome to the Philippines

A country of beautiful islands, diverse cultures, and extraordinary natural wonders.

The Philippines is an archipelagic country in Southeast Asia composed of thousands of islands. It is surrounded by beautiful seas and is known for its white-sand beaches, tropical forests, mountains, waterfalls, and colorful cultural traditions.


More than just a tourist destination, the Philippines is a place where history, culture, nature, and hospitality come together. Every region has its own language, traditions, local dishes, celebrations, and stories.


Whether you want to relax by the sea, explore historical landmarks, experience mountain adventures, or taste authentic Filipino food, the country offers many opportunities for meaningful travel.

Explore Famous Destinations

Discover some of the country's most interesting places and what makes each destination special.

1. Palawan

Palawan is famous for its beautiful limestone formations, clear waters, hidden lagoons, and incredible marine life. El Nido and Coron are popular destinations for nature lovers.

Things to experience: Island hopping, visiting lagoons, snorkeling, and enjoying coastal scenery.

Nature Beaches

2. Boracay

Boracay is known for White Beach, its fine white sand, clear blue waters, and beautiful sunsets. It is one of the country's best-known island destinations.

Things to experience: Swimming, beach walks, sunset viewing, and local dining.

Beach Relaxation

3. Bohol

Bohol is home to the famous Chocolate Hills, the Philippine Tarsier, beautiful beaches, and the Loboc River. It offers both nature and cultural experiences.

Things to experience: Visit the Chocolate Hills, explore the countryside, and enjoy the river scenery.

Nature Wildlife

4. Cebu

Cebu combines historical landmarks, religious heritage, waterfalls, and beautiful coastlines. It is also known for its delicious lechon and vibrant Sinulog Festival.

Things to experience: Visit Magellan's Cross, explore historic churches, and discover natural attractions.

History Adventure

5. Baguio City

Baguio, known as the Summer Capital of the Philippines, is famous for its cool climate, pine trees, parks, flower gardens, and local delicacies.

Things to experience: Visit Burnham Park, Mines View Park, Camp John Hay, and local markets. Try strawberry taho and local products.

Mountains Culture

6. Siargao

Siargao is a tropical island known for its surfing culture, palm-lined roads, lagoons, and peaceful island atmosphere.

Things to experience: Visit the Cloud 9 area, explore Magpupungko Rock Pools during suitable conditions, and discover nearby islands.

Surfing Island Life

7. Banaue, Ifugao

Banaue is known for its magnificent rice terraces shaped into mountain slopes. These landscapes reflect generations of Ifugao knowledge and agricultural traditions.

Things to experience: Appreciate the rice terraces, learn about Ifugao heritage, and visit local communities respectfully.

Heritage Mountains

8. Vigan

Vigan City in Ilocos Sur is famous for its preserved historic streets, ancestral houses, and Spanish colonial-era architecture.

Things to experience: Walk along Calle Crisologo, visit heritage museums, and taste Ilocano delicacies.

History Architecture

9. Albay

Albay is known for the beautiful and recognizable Mayon Volcano, whose symmetrical cone dominates the landscape. The province also has rich Bicolano culture.

Things to experience: Visit Cagsawa Ruins, enjoy local food, and appreciate scenic viewpoints when conditions allow.

Volcano Culture

10. Sagada

Sagada in Mountain Province is known for its mountain scenery, caves, traditional culture, and quiet atmosphere.

Things to experience: Visit local heritage sites, appreciate mountain views, and explore with accredited local guides.

Mountains Heritage

11. Camiguin

Camiguin is a small island province known for its volcanoes, waterfalls, hot and cold springs, and the Sunken Cemetery.

Things to experience: Visit natural springs, enjoy island scenery, and learn about its volcanic history.

Nature Island

12. Davao

Davao is known for its diverse landscapes, Mount Apo, durian, and the Philippine Eagle. It offers urban experiences and nature-based attractions.

Things to experience: Discover local markets, visit nature attractions, and learn about local traditions.

Nature Food

The Three Main Island Groups

The Philippines is geographically divided into Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

LUZON

Luzon is the largest island group and includes Metro Manila, the country's capital region. It is home to historic cities, mountain ranges, rice terraces, beaches, and volcanoes.


Places to discover:

Baguio, Banaue, Sagada, Vigan, Manila, Batanes, Batangas, and Albay.

VISAYAS

The Visayas is a central island group known for beautiful coastlines, historic churches, vibrant festivals, and island communities.


Places to discover:

Cebu, Bohol, Boracay, Iloilo, Bacolod, Siquijor, and Dumaguete.

MINDANAO

Mindanao is known for its mountains, forests, lakes, agricultural communities, and diverse cultural and religious traditions.


Places to discover:

Davao, Camiguin, Siargao, Bukidnon, Cagayan de Oro, and Zamboanga.

Taste the Philippines

Filipino cuisine reflects the country's history, geography, and cultural diversity.

Adobo

A popular Filipino dish commonly prepared with meat, vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and spices.

Sinigang

A comforting sour soup prepared with vegetables and meat or seafood.

Lechon

A traditional roasted pork dish often served during celebrations and special occasions.

Halo-Halo

A colorful dessert made with crushed ice, milk, sweet ingredients, and various toppings.

Ube Delicacies

Purple yam is used in many Filipino desserts such as cakes, pastries, and ice cream.

Local Pastries

Discover regional treats such as Baguio strawberry products, Vigan empanada, and Ilocos bagnet.

Filipino Culture and Traditions

Traveling is also an opportunity to appreciate the people, values, and traditions of the Philippines.

Hospitality

Filipinos are widely known for welcoming visitors warmly and sharing food, stories, and experiences with guests.

Festivals

Festivals express local identity, religious traditions, history, agriculture, and community celebrations.

Languages

Filipino and English are official languages. Many regional languages are also spoken, including Ilocano, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, and Kapampangan.

Traditional Arts

Discover weaving, wood carving, traditional music, dance, textiles, and crafts that reflect local heritage.

Sample Philippine Travel Itineraries

Here are sample travel plans for visitors who want to explore different parts of the country.

3 Days in Baguio City

  • Day 1: Visit Burnham Park, Session Road, and Baguio Cathedral.
  • Day 2: Explore Camp John Hay, Mines View Park, and Botanical Garden.
  • Day 3: Visit local markets, enjoy Cordilleran food, and buy local souvenirs.

4 Days in Cebu

  • Day 1: Explore Cebu City's historical landmarks.
  • Day 2: Discover the natural attractions of southern Cebu.
  • Day 3: Visit coastal communities and enjoy local cuisine.
  • Day 4: Explore local markets and cultural attractions.

4 Days in Palawan

  • Day 1: Arrive and explore the local town.
  • Day 2: Enjoy a guided island-hopping tour.
  • Day 3: Discover lagoons and coastal scenery.
  • Day 4: Visit local shops and prepare for departure.

Important Travel Tips

Prepare carefully so you can enjoy your journey while respecting the places and people you visit.

1. Plan Ahead

Research your destination, transportation, accommodation, and local travel requirements.

2. Check the Weather

Check weather forecasts and official advisories, especially during rainy and typhoon seasons.

3. Prepare a Budget

Plan for transportation, accommodation, meals, entrance fees, and emergency expenses.

4. Respect Local Culture

Follow community guidelines, respect traditions, and ask permission before photographing people.

5. Protect the Environment

Avoid littering, respect wildlife, and follow rules in beaches, forests, and protected areas.

6. Stay Safe

Follow local safety instructions, travel with trusted companions, and use authorized transportation.

7. Support Local Communities

Purchase local products, eat at local restaurants, and support responsible tourism businesses.

8. Keep Important Documents

Secure your identification, travel documents, emergency contacts, and important reservations.
